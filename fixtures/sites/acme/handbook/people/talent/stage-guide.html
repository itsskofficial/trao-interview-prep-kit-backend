How we interview and make offers

Our hiring process has four stages and takes about two weeks.

  1. Recruiter call (30 minutes). Motivation, salary expectations, right to work.
  2. Take-home exercise. A small routing problem. We ask you to spend no more than four hours and we pay for your time.
  3. System design interview (60 minutes). You design a dispatch service with two engineers. We care about trade-offs, not a perfect diagram.
  4. Values interview with the hiring manager (45 minutes). Past situations that show how you work with others.

We make an offer within five working days of the final round and always give feedback.
