How we hire engineers at Initech

We dislike whiteboard puzzles, so our interview process has none.

  1. A 20-minute phone screen with a recruiter.
  2. A 90-minute pair programming session on a real bug from our backlog.
  3. An on-site (or video) day: a debugging interview, a conversation about a system you built, and lunch with the team.

There is no take-home. We send an offer or a no within 48 hours of the final round.
